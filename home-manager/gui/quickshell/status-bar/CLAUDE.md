# CLAUDE.md — status bar layouts (added 2026-11-18, branch `feature/bar-layouts`)

`Theme.barLayout` (Settings > Status bar > Layout; default `balanced`; the layouts are `balanced` and `notch`; classic, islands and the capsule/rule clock looks were removed 2026-11-20) picks the bar; `shell.qml` makes one `Loader` per screen. IPC target `bar` (status-bar config): `setLayout <name>` (in memory, `""` = back to the setting), `cycleLayout`, `layout`.
- **balanced** (`BalancedBar.qml`): full-width bar; left workspaces, centre date and time in `BalancedCentre.qml` (time over date; the unread dot never moves the centred block; click: history panel drops down), right media/wifi/audio/battery/settings. The brightness/volume indicator (`Theme.barOsdStyle`, ~1.2 s) is in the middle: `simple` (default, takes the clock's place), `bubble` (`OsdBubble.qml`, a small glass drop centred on the clock), `dock`, which docks into the bar: its glass gets a bite in the bottom edge of exactly the indicator's shape plus 3 px (`BalancedBar.dock` computes the pill and bite rectangles from the progress `p`; `BarPill.holeRect/holeRadius` cut the glass, `BarDecor.qml` draws the rest: the panel fill without the plugin, the outline of the bite and the bottom line in two pieces), the pill tucked into it; the time stays exactly where it is, the date fades (`BalancedCentre.dateFade`). The cut-out exists only from p >= 0.02, so the idle bar has no gap in its bottom line. `BalancedBar.p` is the animated progress. **Alignment was measured by pixel rows (icon, bar and number within half a physical pixel of each other; `-0.75` offsets are measured corrections).
- **notch** (`NotchBar.qml`): one pill; grows for OSD, a toast, and a control centre (brightness and volume sliders, media, history) when clicked.
- Shared: `BarPill.qml` (compositor glass or opaque panel; the glass is bound to the pill's own geometry because the plugin only hears of that, like `NotificationCard`), `OsdSource.qml` (singleton: brightness from the `events brightnessChanged` IPC via `brightnessctl -m`, volume from Pipewire changes), `Workspaces`, `Media` (MPRIS), `NotificationBell`, `HistoryList`, `OsdBar`, `BarSlider`, `Clock`. The layouts have NO captured glass (nothing without the plugin: an opaque panel).
- **History**: `notifications/shell.qml` keeps the last 50 in memory and writes `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/quickshell-notifications-history.json`; `shared/NotificationHistory.qml` reads it; IPC `notifications markRead | clearHistory | removeFromHistory <seq>`.
- Windows are `barH + 420..520` px tall with `exclusiveZone = barH` and a `mask` of the pills (plus a full-window click catcher while a panel is open).
- Checked (scratch copy beside the real bar, live session): both layouts render with the plugin's glass, OSD morph in all three, toast in islands and notch, history panel in all three, 12 live layout switches with no crash report, history file/IPC on a private bus. NOT checked: mouse clicks (a test-only IPC hook toggled the panels), real brightness keys, volume OSD, MPRIS, the active window title (Hyprland.activeToplevel showed nothing in the test), workspace click dispatch, the settings Layout control, no-plugin fallback, several monitors, `hms` deployment.
- **A Loader resizes the Item it loads, overriding its width/height bindings (a glass pill came out screen-sized): put a pill in an Item that fills the Loader.** The content of a pill needs no `-0.75` correction (that one is for content measured against the bar).
- **Panels of the balanced bar (2026-11-18).** The notification centre, the Wi-Fi menu and the volume menu are one glass panel (`BalancedBar.panel`, `panelKind` history | wifi | audio, `togglePanel(kind, item)`), 6 px under the bar, centred under the clock or the icon (`panelAnchorX`), with the notification shadow. Their content is `WifiPanel.qml` / `AudioPanel.qml` (plain Items; the old `WifiMenu.qml` / `AudioMenu.qml` are now popup-window wrappers around them, used by the notch layout); `Wifi` / `Audio` have `embedded: true` (no popup of their own, `toggled()` instead). The password field of the Wi-Fi panel takes the keyboard through `WlrLayershell.keyboardFocus: Exclusive` while a network is selected (Esc: back, then close). NOT checked: typing a password, connecting, clicks (the test opened the panels through an IPC hook); popup windows could not be tried in a scratch copy (they need an input-focused parent).

- **Foundations (2026-11, branch `feature/popup-foundations`, not yet run).** `BarWindow.qml` is the window of the pill layouts (balanced, notch): layer-shell setup, `barH`, `gap`, `pillH`, `panelH`, `shadowRoom`, `panelOpen` and the toast (`toast`, `toastDuration`); a layout only adds its mask, pills and opening logic. `BarCatcher.qml` is the click-outside item for an open panel (`open`, `dismissed()`). `BarMenu.qml` is the base of the popup-window menus (card, shadow, `grabFocus`): `AudioMenu`, `WifiMenu`, `PowerMenu` are now thin. The notch's `open` is now `panelOpen`.
